      📚 Understand the AQI

      The Air Quality Index turns pollution into one simple number. Tap a level.

      Where does the data come from?

      Air quality comes from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS), which combines satellite observations with atmospheric models. It is served free through Open-Meteo, which also provides the weather data. No account or API key is needed.

      What does "Sensitive mode" do?

      It applies the stricter EPA guidance for people with asthma, lung or heart disease, children, older adults and pregnant people. Activities switch to "caution" once the AQI goes above 50 instead of 100.

      How is the "best time to go outside" picked?

      SkySentry scans the daylight hours of the next day and a half and scores each two-hour slot by forecast AQI, rain chance and how comfortable the temperature feels, then shows you the winner.

      Is this medical advice?

      No. SkySentry gives general guidance from public data. Always follow your doctor's advice and official alerts from local authorities.
